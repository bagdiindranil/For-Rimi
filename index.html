CHAPTER 01

Once, There Were Conversations

It started normally. No grand beginning. No dramatic soundtrack. No narrator announcing: "Something important is about to happen." Just two people talking. Sometimes about something important. Sometimes about absolutely nothing. And somehow... the absolutely nothing conversations were usually the most entertaining ones.

BOY — 8:41 PM Ki korchhish?
GIRL — 8:44 PM Kichu na.
BOY — 8:44 PM Classic answer.
GIRL — 8:45 PM 😂
BOY — 8:47 PM Anyway...

And that's basically how it went. One random conversation would somehow turn into another. Then another. Then somehow... there was always something else to talk about. Nobody really noticed when it became normal. It just did.

CHAPTER 02

THE GIRL
